NOC Issuance Checklist & Guidelines

⚠️ OWNER NOC MANDATORY CHECKLIST

  • ✔
    Clear Account Balance

    Ensure all outstanding bills, consumption charges, and service fees are fully settled.

  • ✔
    Title Deed / Oqood

    Provide a valid copy of the official property Title Deed or Oqood registration certificate.

  • ✔
    Owner Emirates ID / Passport Copy

    A valid copy of the owner's Emirates ID (both sides) or valid Passport copy.

⚠️ TENANT NOC MANDATORY CHECKLIST

  • ✔
    Zero Clearance Certificate

    Provide proof that all current utilities and consumption balances are cleared.

  • ✔
    Tenancy Contract (Ejari / Tawtheeq)

    Provide a valid Ejari or Tawtheeq contract registered under the tenant's name.

  • ✔
    Tenant Emirates ID & Passport

    Valid copy of Emirates ID and Passport with UAE Residence Visa.
