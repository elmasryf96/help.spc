⚠️ OWNER NOC MANDATORY CHECKLIST

  • MOU Verification:

    Ensure the owner has provided a valid, signed MOU (Memorandum of Understanding).

  • New Owner Contact Information:

    Verify and capture the new owner's Mobile Number and Email Address.

  • Interim Billing (No Active Tenant):

    Issue a final invoice for actual chilled water consumption up to today's date if no active tenant exists.

  • Zero Outstanding Balance Rule:

    All active and closed accounts linked to this unit/apartment (including prior owners and previous tenants) MUST have an outstanding balance of exactly 0.00 AED.

📋 TENANT NOC CLEARANCE CHECKLIST

  • Tenant Final Settlement:

    Ensure all outstanding invoices for chilled water consumption up to the move-out date are fully settled.

  • Security Deposit Verification:

    Check security deposit status and clearance as per building specifications.
